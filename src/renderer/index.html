Before you start

This is an unofficial tool

  • It gets your TikTok stream key through Streamlabs’ TikTok access, the same way the Streamlabs Desktop app does. Neither TikTok nor Streamlabs supports using it like this.
  • It may stop working without warning, and TikTok or Streamlabs could restrict an account that uses it. Use it on your own account at your own risk.
  • Your TikTok account needs LIVE access approved for Streamlabs.

Sign in

Connect your TikTok account

Finish signing in in your browser, then come back here.
